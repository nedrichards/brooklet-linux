# Brooklet icon

An open book whose gutter becomes a winding brook. The blue binding retains
Brooklet's water identity; the orange bookmark ties the metaphor to saved
reading and adds a distinctive warm accent.

The production masters are:

- `../../data/icons/scalable/apps/com.nedrichards.brooklet.svg` (128 × 128)
- `../../data/icons/symbolic/apps/com.nedrichards.brooklet-symbolic.svg` (16 × 16)

Both are editable SVGs with no embedded raster images, fonts, external resources,
or filters. Meson already installs them under the configured application ID.

The full-color icon uses the GNOME palette, broad geometric shapes, a four-pixel
front profile, a baseline at y=116, and no shadow outside the silhouette.
The symbolic keeps the open-book and flowing-gutter metaphor while dropping
article lines and bookmark detail.

`preview.png` shows the actual artwork on light and dark backgrounds at 256,
128, 64, and 32 pixels, plus the symbolic at 16 pixels. It is a design review
sheet, not an installed application asset.

Reference: https://developer.gnome.org/hig/guidelines/app-icons.html
